BillWatcher – Reports & Analytics Page Instructions

1. Page Layout
	•	Header (Top Bar):
	•	Title: Reports & Analytics
	•	Right-side action: Export button (CSV/PDF)
	•	Filters (Dropdowns):
	•	Time Range Selector: (Last 3 months, 6 months, Last Year, All Time)
	•	Report Type Selector: (Overview, Monthly Trends, Category Breakdown)

⸻

2. Report Sections

A. Overview (Default View)

Show high-level summary cards:
	•	Total Bills (sum of tracked bills in selected range)
	•	Paid (sum of bills marked paid)
	•	Upcoming (sum of scheduled but unpaid bills)
	•	Overdue (sum of overdue bills)

👉 Each card should use icon + color code for quick scanning:
	•	💰 Total = Blue
	•	✅ Paid = Green
	•	🕒 Upcoming = Yellow/Orange
	•	⚠️ Overdue = Red

⸻

B. Payment Performance

Below the summary, display performance metrics:
	•	Payment Rate (%): (Paid Bills ÷ Total Bills) × 100
	•	Average Monthly Spend: (total ÷ number of months selected)
	•	Total Bills Tracked: raw count

👉 Style:
	•	If payment rate < 50% → Red warning badge
	•	If 50–80% → Yellow caution
	•	If >80% → Green success

⸻

C. Monthly Trends (Graph View)
	•	Use line chart or bar chart to show:
	•	Bills Paid vs Bills Due per month
	•	Add a trend line for “Average Monthly Spend”

👉 Example insight:
“Your bill payments increased by 12% over the last 6 months.”

⸻

D. Category Breakdown (Pie/Donut Chart)
	•	Group bills by category (utilities, rent, credit cards, subscriptions, etc.)
	•	Show percentage & dollar values per category
	•	Add a legend with colors matching chart slices

👉 Example insight:
“Subscriptions make up 25% of your monthly bills.”

⸻

3. Export Functionality
	•	Formats: CSV & PDF
	•	Contents:
	•	Bill name, due date, amount, status (paid/upcoming/overdue), category
	•	Summary totals (same as shown on page)

⸻

4. UX & Styling Notes
	•	Keep cards white with shadows on a light gray background → ensures readability.
	•	Use consistent color coding across all views (green = success, red = urgent).
	•	Allow tap/hover interactions on charts for more detail.
	•	Mobile-first responsive layout (cards stack vertically on small screens).

⸻

5. Future Enhancements (Optional, Later)
	•	AI-powered spending forecast (“You’ll likely spend $X on bills next month”).
	•	Peer comparison insights (“You pay on time more often than 65% of users”).
	•	Nudges (“Paying this bill early could improve your score”).
